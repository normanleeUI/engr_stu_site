# Finding Scholarly Sources

Your two-page IEEE-format paper needs to be built on credible, peer-reviewed sources — not just whatever turns up first in a Google search.
This section helps you understand what makes a source scholarly, shows you where to find engineering research articles, and walks you through evaluating whether a source is trustworthy enough to cite in your paper.
