# Library Use for ECE Students

This guide helps students in ECE 310, ECE 4700 / ME 4810, and ECE 5220 navigate University of Idaho Library resources to complete a two-page IEEE-format research paper.

All three courses share the same research paper assignment, which requires finding and evaluating scholarly sources, citing them in IEEE format, and validating source credibility.
